Project Overview

Poke OS is a collector-focused desktop where exploration can lead to real prizes. Hunt digital card drops, open crates, and earn Collector Crypt collectibles backed by physical cards in a vault.

The hunt is simple: connect a wallet, explore Poke OS, find a live drop, and open it when it is available. Your result is sent to your wallet, where the collectible can be redeemed for the physical card or listed on Collector Crypt.

How The Hunt Works

1. Connect: Pair a compatible wallet so earned collectibles have somewhere to go.

2. Explore: Search the Poke OS desktop for active card drops and hidden crates.

3. Open: Claim a drop when it is available. Some are empty; others contain a real prize.

4. Keep: Your Collector Crypt collectible goes to your wallet. Redeem it for the physical card or trade it on Collector Crypt.

Poke Labs founding principle:

"The best card hunts make exploration feel meaningful. Poke OS connects the joy of finding a hidden drop with real Collector Crypt prizes, and $PokeOS is the signal that keeps the hunt moving."

How Prizes Work

Every prize is a real Collector Crypt collectible backed by a physical card or item in the vault. Your digital collectible can be redeemed for the physical item or listed for sale on collectorcrypt.com.

Prize Tiers:

  • Common: under $30 insured value
  • Uncommon: $30 to $75
  • Rare: $75 to $300
  • Grail: $300 and up

Live odds depend on what is currently in the vault. The prize mix changes as cards are added and claimed.

Fair By Design

Drops are designed to be verifiable. A published seed fingerprint commits each hunt to its result before the drop window begins. The result is calculated from the seed, the drop, your wallet, and a random value from your device, then the seed is revealed so the outcome can be checked.

Fair play matters: hunts are meant for real exploration, not fake locations or automated claiming. Everyone gets the same rules, and suspicious activity may hold a prize for review.

Agentic Systems

Poke OS runs lightweight systems that help discover drops, verify prize records, and route hunt events across the desktop. Each worker has a focused role and publishes structured events to the Poke Terminal and the prize vault.

Active Agents
Scout Agent Finds active card drops and hidden hunt signals across the Poke OS desktop.
Vault Curator Checks prize records, assigns rarity tiers, and keeps vault-backed rewards organized.
Signal Aggregator Routes drop status, wallet events, and hunt results to the right desktop systems.
Archive Librarian Maintains the provenance trail connecting a digital prize to its physical Collector Crypt collectible.
Orchestrator Schedules hunt tasks, retries failed workflows, and routes outcomes to the terminal and UI notifications.

These systems keep the hunt transparent: they prioritize clear drop status, prize rarity, and provenance. The Poke Terminal exposes commands to inspect activity and review hunt events.
